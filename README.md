# UNP_VAC_PROJECT_GRAND_HORIZON_HOSPITALITY_USING_MS_EXCEL
This project was completed under the guidance of UNP Education, focusing on analysing hospitality revenue data using Microsoft Excel. The objective was to understand hotel revenue performance, explore factors influencing profitability, and apply statistical techniques to derive meaningful business insights. The dataset represents a hospitality business scenario involving various data. The project follows an analytical approach from data preprocessing to statistical testing and regression modelling.

 Overview
- Data Preprocessing
  Prepared the dataset by checking duplicates, missing values, inconsistent entries, categorical variables & outliers. The data was standardized & organized for further analysis.
- Exploratory Data Analysis 
Created pivot tables and visualizations to compare hotel classifications, financial quarters, destination types, pricing strategies, and occupancy performance. This helped identify patterns in revenue and business performance across different segments.
- Statistical Hypothesis Testing
Conducted three statistical tests using a 5% significance level:
  One-way ANOVA to compare ADR across hotel classifications.
  Z-test to compare GOP between corporate and non-corporate contract records.
  One-sample proportion Z-test to evaluate occupancy target achievement against a 50% benchmark.
- Correlation Analysis
Used Pearson correlation to examine relationships between ADR, RevPAR, GOP, marketing expenditure, MICE event scores, aviation inflow, housekeeping time & direct room costs.
- Regression Analysis
Developed Simple Linear Regression & Multiple Linear Regression models to analyse RevPAR & evaluate how selected commercial & operational variables relate to hotel revenue performance.

 Key Findings
- Hotel Classification and Pricing
  The ANOVA results showed significant differences in ADR across hotel classifications. Boutique Leisure hotels recorded the highest average ADR at approximately ₹11,770, followed by Upscale Business hotels at ₹10,228, while Express Transit hotels averaged ₹5,501. This highlights how hotel positioning and customer segments influence pricing strategies.
- Corporate Contracts and Profitability
   Hotels with corporate rate contracts recorded an average GOP of ₹412.16 lakh, compared to ₹307.48 lakh for those without contracts. The difference was statistically significant, suggesting that corporate partnerships are associated with stronger operating profitability within the dataset.
- Occupancy Performance
  Approximately 78.17% of the records exceeded their occupancy target. This indicates strong target achievement within the dataset and provides an opportunity to further examine the relationship between occupancy and pricing strategies.
- Revenue and Profitability
   RevPAR and GOP showed a correlation of approximately 0.998, indicating an extremely strong positive relationship. ADR and RevPAR also showed a strong positive correlation of 0.761, reinforcing the importance of room pricing in revenue performance.
- Regression Model Performance
  The multiple linear regression model explained approximately 82.5% of the variation in RevPAR, with an adjusted R² of 82.4%. ADR, aviation passenger inflow, MICE event scores, and OTA advertising expenditure showed positive coefficients, while direct room costs and OTA commission rates showed negative coefficients. This provides a broader understanding of the factors associated with hotel revenue performance
